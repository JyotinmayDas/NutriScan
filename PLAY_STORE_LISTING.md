## Full / Long Description (Max 4,000 characters)

NutriScan is a smart food scanner, calorie counter, and daily nutrition tracker designed for Indian, Bengali, and global diets. Whether your goal is to lose weight, maintain a balanced diet, or build muscle, NutriScan helps you understand what is on your plate and track your daily health habits with ease.

SNAP & ANALYZE YOUR MEALS
Take a photo of your plate or upload a food image from your gallery to estimate portion weights in grams, total calories, and macronutrients (protein, carbohydrates, fats, fiber, and sugar). NutriScan recognizes multi-item plates and home-cooked meals—from Steamed Rice, Roti, Dal, and Rohu Fish Curry (Macher Jhol) to Chicken Biryani, Paneer, Momos, Salads, and international dishes. You can review, adjust portion sizes, or edit any food item before adding it to your daily diary.

COMPREHENSIVE INDIAN, BENGALI & GLOBAL FOOD DATABASE
Search and log meals quickly from a curated food catalog with support for English, Bengali (বাংলা), and Hindi (हिन्दी) food names. Browse dishes by category and log accurate gram portions for Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks.

PERSONALIZED DAILY CALORIE & MACRO GOALS
Set up your personal profile with your age, height, current weight, target weight, activity level, and dietary preference. NutriScan calculates your estimated Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), Body Mass Index (BMI), and daily calorie and macronutrient targets using the Mifflin-St Jeor nutrition equation.

DAILY FOOD DIARY & MEAL PLANNER
• Track daily calories eaten, remaining calories, and macro progress bars for Carbs, Protein, and Fat.
• Copy meals from previous days to log recurring breakfasts or lunches in seconds.
• Generate tailored daily menus based on your dietary style, including Bengali Traditional, Indian Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Vegan, and High-Protein Indian plans.

HYDRATION & WEIGHT PROGRESS TRACKING
• Log your daily water intake in milliliters (ml) with quick-add buttons and customizable hydration goals.
• Record weight entries over time, monitor your BMI status, and view macronutrient distribution reports across Today and Weekly views.

MULTILINGUAL & PRIVACY-FOCUSED
• Switch seamlessly between English, Bengali (বাংলা), and Hindi (हिन्दी).
• Light and Dark Mode themes crafted for comfortable daily use.
• Secure Google Sign-In with per-user cloud synchronization and full account data deletion controls inside Profile & Settings.

MEDICAL & NUTRITIONAL DISCLAIMER
NutriScan is an educational nutrition and wellness tracking tool and is not a medical device. All calorie targets, BMI metrics, visual meal analyses, and meal plans are approximate estimates for informational purposes only and do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified physician or registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet or exercise routine.
